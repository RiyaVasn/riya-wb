Prediction of Drag Coefficient of a Hybrid Body Design of Aircraft

Aerodynamics & CFD

  • Abstract: This paper focuses on evaluating the aerodynamic performance of a hybrid blended-body aircraft configuration through computational fluid dynamics and drag prediction analysis. The study examines how body-shape modifications influence aerodynamic resistance and supports the optimization of aircraft efficiency in the conceptual design stage.
  • Impact: The research connects geometry-driven aerodynamic analysis with practical design decisions for improving flight efficiency in unconventional aircraft concepts.

Generative Design of a Car Transmission System

AIP Conference Proceedings

  • Abstract: This study investigates the application of generative design to a transmission system by examining how topology optimization and design generation strategies can reduce component weight while sustaining stress and durability performance. The work integrates parametric CAD, structural simulations, and design iteration to identify more efficient load-bearing geometries.
  • Impact: It highlights the role of computational design in translating functional performance constraints into lighter, more manufacturable mechanical components.
View Project: Generative Design of a Car Transmission System

Design Optimisation and Additive Manufacturing of an Aircraft Seat

Aerospace Lightweighting & Additive Manufacturing

  • Abstract: This study explores generative design, finite element analysis, and lattice structures to reduce aircraft-seat mass while maintaining structural performance and additive-manufacturing feasibility.
  • Impact: The workflow achieved a weight reduction while placing material according to real aerospace load paths.
