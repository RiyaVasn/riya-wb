About Me

Engineering with curiosity.

I'm Riya Vasan, a mechanical engineer interested in how physical systems, materials, and computational tools come together.

From examining the internal structure of a composite to designing a lighter mechanical component, I enjoy turning complex engineering questions into practical, understandable solutions.

My background

I completed my Master of Applied Science at York University, where my research focused on automated micro-CT image segmentation and void analysis in composite materials. My work connects computer vision with materials characterization to support nondestructive inspection and additive manufacturing.

Through research at York University and an internship at NYU Tandon, I explored how image analysis can reveal what is happening inside a material and help engineers assess its quality.

How I work

My experience spans computational fluid dynamics, structural simulation, CAD, and generative design. My projects include aircraft aerodynamics, lightweight transmission components, and renewable-powered highway infrastructure.

I like working across disciplines: understanding the physical problem, building a model or workflow, and communicating what the results mean. Teaching in a manufacturing lab also strengthened my ability to explain technical concepts and support hands-on learning.

Beyond engineering

After completing my graduate studies, I spent time travelling internationally, volunteering at hostels, and working part-time in travel and tourism. Meeting people from different backgrounds strengthened my communication skills, adaptability, and confidence in unfamiliar environments.

That curiosity carries into my engineering work. I enjoy learning from others, asking thoughtful questions, and finding my way through new challenges.
