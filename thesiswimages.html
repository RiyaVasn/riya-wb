Automated Micro-CT Image Segmentation and Void Analysis

Master's Thesis | York University & NYU Tandon

Non-Destructive Quality Assurance

Traditional destructive testing for composite materials causes excessive waste and permanently destroys components. This makes it inefficient for high-value aerospace and marine parts.

Computer Vision & Unsupervised Automation

An automated inspection framework uses micro-CT imaging and unsupervised machine learning to non-destructively analyze syntactic foams. A neighborhood-guided K-means clustering algorithm segments matrices, particles, and voids without manual training data. The model achieves a 0.92 F1 score for void detection.

Morphological & Spatial Analytics

The pipeline extracts 2D and 3D geometric descriptors to separate process-induced raster defects from hollow glass microsphere cavities. Nearest neighbor distance and spatial orientation tensors quantify void distribution and clustering behaviors across varied manufacturing densities.

Engineering Impact & Results

Geometric and spatial metrics correlate directly with Ultimate Tensile Strength and Young's Modulus. This establishes a scalable digital twin framework for automated defect inspection and process optimization in industrial additive manufacturing.
